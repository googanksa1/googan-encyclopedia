## 6. Qualitative review
- Strengths: no city verdict anywhere. Every criterion is tied to a named data source. It shows real source conflicts (MISA 2020 estimates vs. census 2022) and a real definition mismatch (GOSI-only vs. GOSI + civil service) using official figures. The illustrative example is anonymised (cities A/B/C), every score is declared, and the arithmetic is checked. Knock-out criteria come before scoring, and the sensitivity test shows a real rank flip.
- Remaining gaps: no city-level official figures for land or rent levels; the article deliberately omits them. Labour data is not available at occupation × city level. The MODON count and industrial-city details were not verified from the official site because it blocked fetching. Several logistics facts rest on secondary sources.

## 7. Competitor-gap notes (sample search, limited)
- Searched for Arabic content on location choice and city comparison for Saudi projects. Results were dominated by generic project-idea sites (small-projects.org, projectsegy.com) and academic lecture PDFs listing location factors (Iraqi/Syrian universities, KFUPM chapter). No ertiqa, mada or madroos pages surfaced for this query, so they were not assessed. This is a limitation of the review.
- Gaps observed: (1) location factors are listed without any data source per factor; (2) no weights by project type; (3) no warning about region-vs-city statistics or definition mismatches; (4) no knock-out step linked to Balady licensing; (5) city articles are promotional («لماذا الرياض...») rather than decision tools.
